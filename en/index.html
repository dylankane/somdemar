Som De Mar Events

At Som De Mar, our beautiful seaside setting is the perfect backdrop for life's most important celebrations. We welcome weddings, communions, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate functions, and private parties of all sizes. With the sound of the waves and the sparkle of the Mediterranean as your stage, your guests will enjoy a truly unique and unforgettable atmosphere.

Everything You Need

Our dedicated events team will take care of every detail, from a bespoke Mediterranean menu and carefully paired drinks to music, lighting, and entertainment. Choose from live performances or a professional DJ to keep the energy flowing, and let us coordinate the flow of your event so you can enjoy every moment. At Som De Mar, great food, warm service, and a vibrant setting come together to create memories that last.

Villajoyosa

Located in one of the most picturesque coastal villages of the Costa Blanca, our restaurant is surrounded by history and natural beauty. Enjoy the views of the sea and the charm of our old town, with its colorful fishermen's houses and its unique Mediterranean atmosphere.

How to get here

Situated at the southernmost end of the famous seafront promenade of Villajoyosa, it is very easy to get there. There is abundant parking in the area, including a large public parking lot just two minutes away. The tram station is located 10 minutes away on foot and the town has several bus lines with stops close to the seafront promenade.

Contact Information

We're always happy to hear from our guests. To make a reservation, please call us directly, as our online booking system is still under construction. For any other inquiries or requests, including event planning or large groups, you're welcome to reach out by phone, WhatsApp, email, or via our message feature.
